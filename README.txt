THE [NAME] FILES — QUICK SETUP

1. Open index.html and replace every [NAME] with his name/nickname.
2. Replace the placeholder text wherever you want.
3. Put your drawing in assets/artwork.png.
4. Put your video in assets/birthday-video.mp4.
5. Replace the FINAL_MESSAGE text with YOUR OWN words.
6. For screenshots, replace the four placeholder boxes in index.html with image files later.
7. Upload the whole folder to a GitHub repository and enable GitHub Pages.

The design is intentionally lightweight and works on phones.
